[b]Q: Does this multiply my Reflex Attribute duration bonus?[/b]
A: Yes! As of v2.1, the math daemon multiplies your total duration naturally through the engine's stat pipeline.

[b]Q: Why does the cooldown slider stop at 0.1x?[/b]
A: That's 90% reduction. If we went to 0.0x (0 seconds), the engine animation state machine tends to freak out. 0.1x is basically instant anyway—don't be greedy, choom.

[b]Q: Does this affect enemies with Sandevistans?[/b]
A: Negatory. This script injects directly into the [i]PlayerPuppet[/i] stats system. Adam Smasher and those pesky Wraiths don't get free upgrades or your penalties. They stay slow; you decide how fast you go.

[b]Q: Can I change settings mid-combat?[/b]
A: You're crazy, but yes. Open the menu, slide the bar, close the menu. The next time you activate the Sandy, the new stats apply.

[b]Q: Do I need a fresh save?[/b]
A: Nope. Load up your level 60 character and start zooming. No new game required.

## Version 2.1.0 Changelog (For Nexus "Changelogs" Entry)

```text
Added bidirectional Duration Multiplier (0.1x to 10.0x), enabling short tactical burst challenge mode (<1.0x) or extended duration (>1.0x).
Added bidirectional Cooldown Multiplier (0.1x to 5.0x), enabling accelerated recharge (<1.0x) or extended cooldown penalties (>1.0x).
Setting sliders to 1.0x now cleanly detaches custom modifiers, leaving the player in 100% vanilla state.
Migrated to ScriptableSystem with isolated modifier handles, fixing native Reflex duration scaling (+0.1s/reflex) and preserving other perks/stats.
Optimized engine performance by removing busy polling loops and synchronizing stats automatically upon session load.
Added automatic backwards-compatible migration for legacy settings.json configurations.
Removed redundant custom reset button in favor of Native Settings UI's built-in default reset.
```
